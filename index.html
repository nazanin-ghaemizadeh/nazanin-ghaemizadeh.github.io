Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Systems Optimization at Sharif University of Technology, supervised by Prof. Kourosh Eshghi.

I received my Master's degree in Engineering Management from Sharif University of Technology, under the supervision of Dr. Moslem Habibi and the advisory of Dr. Mehdi Seif-Barghy.

I also hold a Bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering from Alzahra University, where I was supervised by Maryam Eghbali-Zarch.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Optimization, Data-driven Decision-making, and Machine Learning. I am particularly interested in how data and intelligent algorithms can be integrated with optimization models to solve complex, real-world problems under uncertainty.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss research or explore potential collaboration!
